How it works

Three simple steps from your screen to the tree.

✏️

1. Design it

Pick a design, type the name and choose your colours. What you see in the preview is what gets printed.

🖨️

2. We print it

Every ornament is 3D-printed to order and checked by hand before it's packed.

🎁

3. Pick up or post

Collect locally on the Sunshine Coast, or have it posted to you. We'll confirm your order and payment details by email.

Questions

How big are the ornaments?

The bauble is about 80 mm across, the disc 76 mm, the star 86 mm wide and the Christmas tree is about 86 mm wide and 99 mm tall, all around 4 mm thick. The family tree grows with the number of names, roughly 60–120 mm tall. They're all light enough for any tree branch.

How do I pay?

Once you place an order we'll email you to confirm the details and send an invoice. Nothing is charged online.

How does the free ornament deal work?

Can I add the year?

Yes, on every design and at no extra cost. Tick "Add the year" in the designer, or leave it off for an ornament that suits any Christmas.

Can I order a set for the whole family?

Yes. Click "Add another name" in the designer and each name becomes its own ornament in the same design and colours.

What are they made from?

They're printed in PLA, a durable plant-based plastic. Keep them away from heat, like a hot car dashboard.
